# Standards Registry

This registry is metadata for future verified calculation profiles. Listing a document here does not mean the current V1 implements or certifies compliance with it.

Initial categories to verify and map before construction-grade modules are enabled:

- Vietnam building planning/legal constraints applicable to the exact locality and project.
- Loads and actions for building structural design.
- Reinforced concrete structural design.
- Foundation and geotechnical design.
- Electrical installations, protection and earthing.
- Internal water supply and drainage.
- Fire/life-safety requirements where applicable.
- Energy, ventilation and air-conditioning requirements where applicable.

For every implemented profile, store exact document identifier, edition, amendments, applicable clauses, formula mapping, test cases, reviewer, and review date.
